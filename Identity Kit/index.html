Style note

Space Grotesk for headings, Inter for body. Near-black #1B1F27 on near-white #FAF8F5, with burnt orange #D9531E as the one loud color and muted slate #4F6D7A used sparingly for secondary emphasis. Mood: quietly confident and structural — geometry doing the talking, never louder than the work it frames.
